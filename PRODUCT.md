## Platform

## Users

Potential clients and collaborators. They should be able to understand Fabrizio's work, see the range from professional projects to personal interests, and find a clear way to connect.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for presenting software and personal projects, plus short notes and longer blog style entries. Success means a visitor can quickly find relevant work and a contact path.

## Positioning

A portfolio and writing archive that brings software work, modeling, and hands-on projects together. The audience and content mix are confirmed; the homepage visual direction is user-selected and documented in its surface brief.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The site is a Jekyll static site published through GitHub Pages at destro.me. Existing collections and routes cover projects, making, modeling, and posts. The audience is potential clients and collaborators; writing should support both short notes and longer entries. English is the confirmed homepage language. No specific contact address was provided, so existing GitHub and LinkedIn links are the contact paths on hand.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing project data in `_data/projects/`, project entries in `_projects/`, post files in `_posts/`, and preview images in `assets/images/`. The homepage introduces Fabrizio as a Senior Software Engineer interested in Cyber Security, especially endpoint security. The first homepage version hides project tiles while project routes remain available. Existing draft posts remain unpublished; do not invent published entries, dates, clients, or project outcomes.

## Product Principles

- Help potential clients and collaborators find relevant work and connect.
- Present software and personal projects together.
- Let project artifacts and images carry the story.
- Support concise notes and longer written entries.
- Keep published content factual and grounded in existing material.
